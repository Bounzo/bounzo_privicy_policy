Bounzo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Bounzo ("Bounzo", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the Bounzo mobile game.

Developer: Bounzo
Privacy contact: bounzogame@gmail.com

1. Information We Process

Bounzo may process certain information through the game itself and through third-party services used to provide game functionality, advertising, purchases, and game-score synchronization.

Depending on the features you use, this may include game scores, game progress, game settings, device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and information required to process purchases.

Bounzo does not require users to create a Bounzo account or provide their name, address, telephone number, or payment card details directly to Bounzo.

2. Google Play Games Services

Bounzo uses Google Play Games Services to provide game-score related functionality.

For example, Bounzo may use Google Play Games Services to save or synchronize a player's score associated with their Google Play Games profile.

Bounzo does not provide a leaderboard or achievement system.

Google may process information associated with Google Play Games Services in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms. Bounzo does not receive or store your Google account password.

3. Local Game Data

Bounzo may store certain gameplay information locally on your device. This may include game progress, settings, preferences, and other information required for the game to function correctly.

Local game data may be removed if you uninstall Bounzo or clear the application's data through your device settings.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

Bounzo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process information such as an advertising ID, app set ID, IP address, device information, app interactions, diagnostic information, and other information depending on the configuration and features used.

This information may be used for purposes including delivering and measuring advertisements, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and improving advertising services.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent mechanisms may be used before personalized advertising or other applicable processing takes place.

For more information about Google's handling of data, please review Google's privacy policy and Google AdMob documentation.

5. In-App Purchases

Bounzo uses Google Play Billing to provide optional in-app purchases.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Bounzo does not directly collect or store your credit card, debit card, bank account, or other payment card information.

Google Play may provide Bounzo with information necessary to verify a purchase and provide the purchased digital content.

6. Restoring Purchases

Bounzo may provide a feature that allows eligible purchases previously made through Google Play to be restored.

Purchase restoration is performed using Google Play Billing information necessary to verify the user's eligible purchases.

Bounzo does not store payment card information.

7. How Information Is Used

Information processed through Bounzo and its third-party services may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining the game.
  • Saving and synchronizing game scores.
  • Saving local game progress and settings.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Processing and restoring in-app purchases.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized transactions.
  • Improving game stability and performance.

8. Information Sharing and Third-Party Services

Bounzo uses third-party services to provide certain features. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The services used by Bounzo may include:

  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

Information may be transmitted to these providers when necessary to provide their respective services.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Bounzo and its associated services.

Information transmitted by supported Google services may be protected using appropriate security technologies, including encryption in transit where provided by the relevant service.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Bounzo retains local game data on the device for as long as it is needed to provide game functionality, unless the user removes the data.

Information processed by Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob is handled and retained according to the applicable policies and retention practices of those services.

Because Bounzo does not provide a separate Bounzo account system, Bounzo does not maintain a separate Bounzo user account database.

11. Children's Privacy

Bounzo is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. Bounzo does not knowingly request children under 13 to provide personal information directly to Bounzo.

Third-party services integrated into Bounzo may have their own requirements concerning children and advertising. Bounzo is configured and operated in accordance with applicable Google Play and Google service requirements.

12. Your Choices

You may control certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and through Google's available privacy and advertising controls.

You may also remove locally stored Bounzo data by uninstalling the game or clearing its application data through your device settings.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Bounzo's features, services, third-party integrations, or applicable legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.
