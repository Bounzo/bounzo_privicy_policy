Bounzo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bounzo ("the Game") handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Bounzo may process certain information through third-party services used by the Game. Depending on the features you use, this may include game scores, game progress, device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information.

2. Google Play Games Services

Bounzo uses Google Play Games Services for game-related features such as scores, achievements, leaderboards, and game progress where applicable. Information associated with Google Play Games may be processed by Google to provide these services.

Bounzo does not request or store your Google account password. Google handles Google Play Games account authentication and related account information.

3. Local Game Data

Bounzo may store certain game data locally on your device, such as game progress, settings, or other gameplay information. This local data is used to provide and maintain game functionality.

4. Advertising

Bounzo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes including advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

Where required by applicable law, users may be asked to provide consent for personalized advertising or other applicable data processing.

5. In-App Purchases

Bounzo uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play. Bounzo does not directly collect or store your credit card, debit card, or other payment card information.

Google Play Billing may provide purchase information necessary for Bounzo to verify purchases and provide purchased items.

6. Purchase Restoration

Bounzo may provide a feature that allows eligible previous purchases to be restored. Purchase restoration is performed through Google Play Billing. Bounzo does not store your payment card information.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed through Bounzo and its third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide game functionality and save game progress.
  • Provide scores, achievements, and leaderboard features.
  • Display advertisements.
  • Process and restore in-app purchases.
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Improve the stability and performance of the Game.

8. Third-Party Services

Bounzo uses third-party services including Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through Bounzo. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data stored locally on your device may be removed by uninstalling the Game or clearing its application data.Data processed by Google services is handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies and retention practices.

11. Children's Privacy

Bounzo is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page together with an updated "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Bounzo's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Bounzo Google Play Store listing.
